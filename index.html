    The Pulsar

    A neutron star spins at the centre of the system. Its beam keeps a steady beat — that beat is also the rhythm of the soundtrack. Match your fire to the pulse and the field will reward you.

    Combo & Halo

    Consecutive on-beat hits build your combo. As the combo climbs the halo music layers in, the score multiplier rises, and pilot's-log entries unlock. A missed beat or a hit taken collapses the chain.

    Canisters & Crystals

    Asteroids sometimes drop canisters — fly over them to claim a powerup: trident spreads three shots, pierce cuts through, shield absorbs one hit, slow-mo dilates time, radar widens your firing arc. Gold crystals reward a clean rhythm hit with a fresh canister.
